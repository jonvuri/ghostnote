## Prepared group route

The bounded route reads top-level roots and direct child banks. It requires
exactly one owner-UUID master row with both group flags false. It excludes
that row from membership, then requires a closed forest, one parent per

within the callback sequence. Missing, duplicate, or malformed master rows
