The audit also inspected all eight available non-analysis rows with no parsed
notes. Six compact rows had incomplete metadata or unsupported row fields. One
MusicXML row omitted the side ledger. One MusicXML row returned JSON instead of
XML and omitted the ledger. These are model outcomes. The scorer recorded the
lost components.

All retained prompt, task, candidate, and response hashes match. Every frozen
score reproduces. The audit found no prompt, reference, format-adapter, parser,
scorer, aggregation, provider-identity, schedule, or cost error in a retained
row.

The audit found one run-policy defect. The inherited runner stops after three
unavailable responses. The v5 written stopping rule omitted this active rule.
Claude stopped at sequence 138 after three output-limit responses. Keep its 135
available rows. Do not treat its unattempted rows as failures.

Gemini stopped correctly on one HTTP 503 at sequence 564. It retained 563
available rows. OpenAI completed all 592 rows.
