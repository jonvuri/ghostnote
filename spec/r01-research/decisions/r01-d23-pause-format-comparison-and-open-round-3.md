# R01-D23 — Pause the format comparison; open round 3 on real-use follow-ups

Date: 2026-10-31. Status: **accepted by the operator** ("Let's pause the
format comparison at the development stage indeed, packaging up its useful
results to be easy to find and reference later … However, keep R01 open, and
launch round 3, investigating your recommendations #2 and #3 … in more depth
immediately. In addition to those two, I also want to investigate the timing
overlays more specifically.").

## Context

[E392](../../../context/evidence/experiments/e392-r01-real-usage-by-capability-cell.md)
classified 11 real product sessions on the
[R01-D22](r01-d22-capability-grid-focus.md) grid. Seventeen of 20 note-write
interactions were A1/B1 scripted generation; three were single-turn B2
transforms; B3 never occurred. No failure was caused by the notation. The
largest costs came from tool design, guidance and host identity. R01's measured
C3/C4 differences concern inline A3 authoring, which real use did not exercise.
The [reassessment](../../../context/work/r01-format-comparison.md#real-usage-and-holistic-reassessment-2026-10-31)
recommended concluding the comparison at the development stage.

## Decision

1. **Pause the format comparison at the development stage.** Round 2's step 4
   is resolved as neither: no rigorous round and no stronger cohort now. The
   live format stays; C4 remains a documented candidate for inline group
   authoring. The packages, harnesses and resume notes stay intact so the
   comparison can restart.
2. **Package the comparison's results** in one citable summary,
   [FINDINGS.md](../FINDINGS.md).
3. **Keep R01 open and start round 3** with three offline investigations of
   E392's findings:
   - *Tool-design costs:* slot probing, verification re-reads, read-to-file
     use, missing structural and selector operations, and related items.
   - *Mid-turn ID re-mint:* whether the seven re-minted clips reflect an agent
     mistake, a Ghostnote defect or host behaviour.
   - *Timing overlays:* what they contained, and whether they represented the
     operator's timing instructions or followed reference guidance by default.

## Consequences

- R01's outcome on adoption, claims and naming no longer waits for round 2.
  Round 3's findings inform claims (overlay policy) and the tool surface.
- Live checks that round 3 proposes need the usual operator consent.
